# Headshot prompt

No image tool is connected to this session, so I couldn't make the photo myself. Paste the prompt below into an image tool of your choice (for example ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney) and **attach your own photo**.

---

> Create a clean, professional LinkedIn headshot of the person in the attached photo. Keep their real face, features, skin tone and hairstyle exactly as they are. Do not change how they look. Shoulders-up framing, facing the camera, a natural friendly expression. Plain neutral light-grey background. Soft, even, natural light with no harsh shadows. Clothing suited to an engineer in pharma or lab R&D: a plain collared shirt or a simple dark blazer. Square image, sharp focus, realistic photograph, no filters or retouching beyond basic lighting.

---

**Notes**
1. LinkedIn says your profile photo must look like you. If a tool changes your face, don't use the result.
2. Use a square crop of at least 400 x 400 pixels, as a JPG or PNG under 8 MB.
3. Your photo goes to whichever image tool you use, so read that tool's privacy terms first.
